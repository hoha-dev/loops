Acceptable for one developer; a team wants `owns-guard` to allow only new files under `docs/decisions/` plus `OPEN.md`.
- **F4 · PLAUSIBLE · MINOR · open** — `stage-guard`'s regex blocks `git add .` but not `git add -- .` or `git add ./`; the fixture does not cover those spellings. Recommendation: extend the pattern and the fixture (claim C4 in the predecessor's plan: watch for agents routing around a guard).
